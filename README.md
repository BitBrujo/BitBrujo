# Hey, Hey

I'm Joaquin, a design engineer and creative technologist in NYC. I work where design and engineering overlap, taking ideas from the first coded prototype to production. I also run [Hypno Labs](https://hypnolabs.io), a studio working at the edge of the agentic web.

Before software I spent thirteen years cutting documentaries and TV for HBO, VICE, PBS, NBC and A&E.

## Now ->

[**Motley NYC**](https://motley.nyc) A school and neighborhood guide for NYC families that weighs transit, safety and school fit against what a family actually cares about. I built it solo: 63 routes, a 68-table geospatial schema, 45 background jobs and an in-app agent with its own eval harness. Its [MCP server](https://github.com/HypnoLabs-io/motley-mcp) is public and needs no key, so Claude or ChatGPT can answer NYC school and admissions questions straight from Motley's data.

[**Loopcraft**](https://www.loopcraft.io) My newsletter, on true individual power in the age of AI.

## Open source ->

[**Rigger**](https://github.com/BitBrujo/rigger) A visual dashboard for testing and debugging Claude Agent SDK agents. Configure an agent and its tools, then watch the session stream with live cost tracking, without writing code.

[**passport-proof**](https://github.com/HypnoLabs-io/passport-proof) Ed25519-signed identity passports for AI agents. Anyone can verify one offline, even after the issuer has gone away.

[**l402-ts**](https://github.com/HypnoLabs-io/l402-ts) L402 for TypeScript: charge for HTTP requests over Bitcoin Lightning. The verification core is edge-safe.

Both libraries were pulled out of **Agent Church** (2026, now retired), an experiment in treating AI agents as paying customers. A philosopher interviewed each agent and wrote its SOUL.md, and the agent paid for whatever came next over Lightning or USDC. Seven agents paid on their own. Every one of them had been sent by a person.
